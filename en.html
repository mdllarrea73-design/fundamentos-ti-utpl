Resources

Bibliography and open educational resources from the course plan. Virtual library resources may require UTPL login.

  • Required bibliography · available in the EVA Rodríguez, G. (2026). Guía virtualizada Fundamentos de Tecnologías de la Información. Editorial UTPL.

    Course content, reinforcement activities, and self-assessments for each unit.

  • Supplementary bibliography · UTPL Virtual Library Laudon, K. y Romero, A. (2012). Sistemas de información gerencial. Pearson.

    Useful for weeks 2 to 4: evolution of IT infrastructure and milestones in the history of the Internet.

  • Supplementary bibliography · UTPL Virtual Library Stewart, J. M., Chapple, M. y Gibson, D. (2012). CISSP Study Guide. Wiley.

    Provides further study of information security and certification in this field.

  • Open educational resource · Prezi Presentación interactiva: las tecnologías de la información, su importancia y componentes
  • Open educational resource · YouTube video Software: definition and development, software engineering
